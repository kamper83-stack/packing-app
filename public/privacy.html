PackPlanner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

PackPlanner ("the app", "we") helps travelers build a smart packing list for a trip. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It is written to match what the application actually does.

1. Data we collect

DataWhy
Email address Account identity and sign-in.
Password (stored only as a one-way hash) Authentication. We never store your password in plain text.
Trip details — destination, start/end dates, airline, number and composition of travelers, luggage counts, trip type, and the generated packing list To generate and save your packing plan.
Weather forecast cached with a trip To show packing-relevant conditions for your destination.

We do not collect precise location, microphone, or camera data, and the app contains no advertising.

2. Third-party services

To produce suggestions and forecasts, trip details you enter are sent to the following services. Your email and password are never sent to them.

ServiceWhat is sentPurpose
Google Gemini (generative AI) Destination, dates, traveler counts, trip type, luggage counts Generate the packing-list suggestions
Google weather forecast API Destination and trip dates Retrieve the forecast for your destination
Skyscanner Flights (via RapidAPI) Route (origin/destination) and dates Optional flight lookups

Each provider processes this data under its own privacy policy. We send only the trip fields each feature needs, never account credentials.

3. Data retention

Your account and trips are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Weather forecasts are cached alongside the trip they belong to.

4. Your rights — access and deletion

You can view and delete individual trips from within the app. You may request deletion of your entire account and all associated trips and packing items; self-service account deletion is being added to the app. Until it ships, email the contact below and we will delete your account and its data.

5. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. API responses containing your data are never stored in the browser's offline cache.

6. Children

PackPlanner is a general-audience travel utility and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: privacy@packing.erankam.dev.
